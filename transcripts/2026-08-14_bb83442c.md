### 🤖 Claude

Progress committed as `743a31d`, with a fully green `lake build`. Here's what changed:

## What I did

**Found and fixed a soundness bug in `supersetRemoval`.** The old definition removed any polygon that had another list member as a subset. But `ConvexPolygon.isSubsetOf` isn't antisymmetric — two distinct polygons (e.g. cyclically rotated vertex lists of the same polygon) can each contain the other — so the symmetric filter would delete *both* members of such a pair, potentially removing the last witness the soundness invariant needs. `Sound.supersetRemoval` was unprovable as stated. The new definition in [Operations.lean](Moser/Manipulation/Operations.lean) processes the list as a sequential fold (`supersetRemovalStep`) that always keeps a representative.

**Proved four leaf sorries of the lower-bound spine** in [LowerBound.lean](Moser/LowerBound.lean):
- `Sound.supersetRemoval` — via a fold invariant: whenever a witness is dropped, a polygon contained in it is retained in its stead.
- `le_moserCoverNumber_of_forall_convex_cover` — `le_sInf`, then the placement hull covers each worm through the inverse placement.
- `initialWorm_isWormHull` — the L-shaped unit path `(0,½) → (0,0) → (½,0)` parametrized by arc length as `t ↦ (max 0 (t−½), max 0 (½−t))`, with its 1-Lipschitz bound, exact range (union of the two legs), and hull computation down to the triangle's three vertices.
- `exists_pinnedSmallCover` — un-pinning: move the cover onto the L-shaped worm; convexity, coverage (isometries compose), and volume (direct isometries are measure-preserving; convex sets are null-measurable) all survive.

**Added the Invariant-2 preservation lemmas** `ContainsInitialWorm.{bigSetRemoval, supersetRemoval, cleanup}`, which the blueprint stated but Lean lacked, and updated [content.tex](blueprint/src/content.tex) (new `supersetRemoval` description; both pruning propositions now `\leanok`).

## Where the spine stands now

Remaining sorries in `LowerBound.lean`, in rough order of advisability:
1. `realHull_subset_realHull` (containment bridge) — needs soundness of `toHalfSpaces`/`contains` from `Polygon.lean`, which has its own sorries.
2. `volume_realHull` (area bridge) — shoelace area vs. Lebesgue measure; substantial but self-contained.
3. `Sound.wormAdding` — its docstring explicitly warns not to prove it before computationally validating the discretization, so I left it.
4. The final theorem — awaits a certificate run of the algorithm.

I also saved project notes to memory (spine status, build gotchas, the Mathlib lemma names that took searching) so future sessions can pick up faster.

## What happened this session

**`volume_realHull`** — the Lebesgue measure of a rational polygon's real hull equals its shoelace area — is now a theorem, built in three layers:

1. **Diagonal positivity unblocked everything** (`fan_diag_pos`). The blocker I'd identified — every vertex lies strictly left of every diagonal from vertex 0, which linear cocycle manipulations provably can't reach — falls to a different observation: *the angular order around a vertex is transitive inside an open half-plane*, and that transitivity is an instance of the Grassmann–Plücker relation `[dv][ut] = [du][vt] + [dt][uv]`, a pure `ring` identity plus sign reasoning (`rcross_trans`). Chaining consecutive fan triangles through it yields all diagonals. This same technique should crack the long-sorried `cyclicCCWChain_gap_ccw`-family in [Polygon.lean](Moser/Geometry/Polygon.lean) — I noted that in memory.
2. **Fan additivity** (`volume_convexHull_fan`): the hull *is* the union of fan triangles (the membership induction strengthened to land in the union), triangles are pairwise a.e.-disjoint (separated by diagonal lines, each null as a proper affine subspace), so the volume is the sum of `volume_triangle` values.
3. **Shoelace telescoping**: the `shoelaceArea` foldl rewritten as an indexed sum (`foldl_shoelace_aux`), the fan sum telescoped into the cyclic sum (`fan_sum_eq_cyclic`), and the whole thing instantiated at the polygon via a single ℕ-indexed reformulation of the `IsCCWPolygon` invariant, with casts pushed through.

The blueprint gained `lem:area-bridge` with a proof sketch.

## Where the spine stands

Everything below the double line is proven; the record bound `areaThreshold ≤ moserCoverNumber` now rests on exactly two things:

1. **`Sound.wormAdding`** — deliberately deferred: its docstring warns the discretization's quantitative claims (grid resolution vs. shrink margin, `ofList` candidate survival) must be validated computationally first, and the statement may need extra hypotheses.
2. **The certificate run** — exhibiting a terminating working-set iteration (`∃ s, s.Sound ∧ s.polygons = []`).

So the natural next step is no longer proving — it's **running the algorithm**: `#eval` experiments with `addWormAndCleanup` to validate `wormReplacement`'s discretization and see whether the working set actually empties, which would simultaneously de-risk `wormAdding`'s statement and produce the certificate. ## What the conversion involved

Per the reference's migration path, all 19 library files (18 modules + the `Moser.lean` root) now use the module system:

- **`module`** header, **`public import`** for all imports, and the whole file body wrapped in **`@[expose] public section … end`**. This is the maximal-compatibility form the guide recommends starting from — everything the project exported before is still exported and unfoldable downstream. (Your existing `private` lemmas stay private, and proofs are now automatically private per module semantics.)

Only two files needed real adaptation, both instructive:

1. **`native_decide` runs at the meta phase** under modules: it compiles the decision procedure, which needs the *compiled code* of the definitions and `Decidable` instances it evaluates — not just their types. So [Constants.lean](Moser/Constants.lean) and [LowerBound.lean](Moser/LowerBound.lean) gained `public meta import` lines for `Mathlib` and the relevant `Moser.Geometry.Polygon`/`Operations` chain (the error messages name the missing modules exactly, which made this mechanical).
2. **ProofWidgets values are meta declarations**, so every definition in [PolygonWidget.lean](Moser/Geometry/PolygonWidget.lean) (`palette`, `frameForBox`, `listToHtml`, …) is now a `meta def`, with dual-phase (`public import` + `public meta import`) imports of the geometry it renders — exactly the pattern the reference describes for metaprograms that consume non-meta definitions.

`Visualize.lean` and `Scratch.lean` were left as plain source files: they're not part of the library target, and plain files can freely import modules.

## One follow-up worth knowing about

The current conversion is deliberately maximal (`@[expose] public section` everywhere). The module system's payoffs — faster incremental builds, sealed proof internals, smaller public interfaces — grow as you *minimize*: dropping `@[expose]` where downstream code never unfolds a definition, demoting `public import`s used only in proofs to plain `import`s, and narrowing the public sections. That's an incremental cleanup that can be done file-by-file whenever convenient; the build will flag anything that was actually needed. I noted the house pattern in memory so future files follow it.

### 🤖 Claude

Minimization done — committed as `d4c2b25`, build fully green. What changed and what deliberately didn't:

## Tightened

- **Sealed six files** (`@[expose] public section` → `public section`): [LowerBound.lean](Moser/LowerBound.lean), [AllowableAdditions.lean](Moser/Geometry/AllowableAdditions.lean), `Worm/Basic`, `Worm/Enumeration`, `Sorried/PolygonContainment`, and `PolygonWidget`. Their declarations stay public, but their definition bodies are now module-internal — nothing downstream unfolds them, and the build confirms it. For `LowerBound` in particular this is the module system working as intended: the spine's theorems are exported while all its proof machinery (barycentric lemmas, fan induction, Plücker transitivity) is sealed behind the interface.
- **Demoted `public meta import` → `meta import`** in Constants and LowerBound: the `native_decide`-compiled auxiliary declarations live inside proofs, which are always private, so the meta scope doesn't need re-exporting.
- **Demoted Operations' unused `Worm.Basic` import** from public to plain (it's referenced nowhere in the file — a candidate for outright removal if you like).

## Deliberately kept exposed

The computational geometry layer — `RationalPoint` through `Polygon`, `PolygonArea`, `Constants`, `Invariants`, `Operations`, `DirectIsometry.*`, `GridEpsilonNet`, `CompactnessOutline`. These aren't conservatism: downstream proofs genuinely rewrite with and unfold their definitions (`LowerBound` rewrites `moserCoverNumber`/`minimalVolume`, unfolds `contains`/`toHalfSpaces`/`shoelaceArea`, `norm_num`s through `InitialWorm`), and the next big goal — proving `Sound.wormAdding` — will need `wormReplacement`/`discretizeIsometries` definitionally. Sealing them now would just force re-exposure later.

The rationale is recorded in the commit message and in my project notes, so future files (and the eventual certificate module) follow the same pattern.
